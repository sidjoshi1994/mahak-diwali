🍬 Diwali around the office & home

How Diwali is Celebrated

Rangoli

Colourful patterns drawn by hand with coloured powder at the front door.

Diyas

Small clay oil lamps on every doorstep, window and balcony.

Traditional clothing

Everyone wears something new: kurtas, sarees and lehengas in bright colours.

Sweets

Ladoo, kaju katli and barfi, gifted by the box to friends and neighbours.

🌍 Diwali across the world

Diwali Beyond India

UAE

Dubai’s Festival of Lights runs 2–12 November, with music, rangoli workshops and fireworks.

India

Ayodhya lines its riverbanks with millions of diyas and keeps setting world records.

Singapore

Little India’s streets glow with lights for weeks, and Deepavali is a public holiday.

United Kingdom

London’s Trafalgar Square hosts a free Diwali festival, and Leicester holds one of the biggest outside India.
